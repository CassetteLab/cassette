Cassette 1.12

NEW
• Sort a playlist's songs — a sort control in the playlist toolbar orders the list by title, artist, recently added or release date, and "Playlist Order" puts it back. The sort is display-only: it never changes the playlist on your server, and it is remembered per playlist. Recently Added and Release Date need the server, so they are hidden offline.
• macOS: ⌥← and ⌥→ skip back and forward 10 seconds. ⌘F now actually focuses the search field — it did nothing before.
• Settings: clear the artwork cache. Covers saved with your downloads are deliberately kept, so clearing never costs you artwork offline.

FIXED
• macOS: the bottom of every list is usable again. A strip above the player bar belonged to no scroll view and answered neither click nor scroll wheel.
• Long tracks download properly. The download session capped the whole transfer at 30 seconds, so anything that took longer failed no matter how good the connection was.
• Failed downloads now tell you. Before, a download that failed logged nothing and showed nothing — the row simply reverted, indistinguishable from one that never started.
• Missing artwork on downloaded music is repaired automatically, once per launch when the server is reachable. A cover saved with a download is only written at download time, and an earlier cleanup removed some of them.
• Faster song lists on large libraries. The list ran one blocking database query per visible row for favourites, and re-read every downloaded track on each refresh.

PLEASE TEST
• Playlist sorting, non-regression first: open a playlist and confirm it looks exactly as it did in 1.11 before touching the sort. Then sort it, and check that tapping a row plays that row.
• Playlist sorting, the important one: with a sort active, add songs to the playlist, then switch back to "Playlist Order". The playlist's own order must be unchanged — the sort must never be written to the server. Same check after removing a track while sorted.
• Editing is intentionally disabled while a sort is active. Switch back to "Playlist Order" and confirm the pencil (iOS) and drag-to-reorder (macOS) come back.
• Download a long track — 20 minutes or more. Then try one that cannot work (stop your server mid-download) and confirm you now get an error instead of silence.
• Artwork in airplane mode: with music downloaded, clear the artwork cache in Settings, turn off the network, and confirm your downloaded albums still show their covers.
• macOS layout: resize the window and scroll to the bottom of a few lists — the last row must be clickable, both while playing and with nothing playing. Album, playlist and artist screens changed the most.
• macOS shortcuts: ⌥← / ⌥→ during playback, and that they are greyed out on a radio stream. ⌘F should put the caret in the search field.
